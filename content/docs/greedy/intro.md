# Greedy Technique

The greedy technique builds up a solution piece by piece, always choosing the next piece that offers the most obvious and immediate benefit.

### Characteristics
- **Locally Optimal**: Choices are made without looking ahead.
- **Irrevocable**: Choices cannot be changed once made.
- **Heuristic**: May not always find the global optimum.
