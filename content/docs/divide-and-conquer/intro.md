# Divide and Conquer

Unlike decrease-and-conquer, divide-and-conquer involves splitting the problem into several subproblems of the same type, solving them independently, and then combining their solutions.

### Steps
1. **Divide**: Split the problem.
2. **Conquer**: Solve subproblems (recursively).
3. **Combine**: Merge solutions.
