# Middle School Procedure

The prime factorization method is the procedure typically taught in middle school for finding the GCD of two numbers.

### Procedure
1. Find the prime factorization of $m$.
2. Find the prime factorization of $n$.
3. Identify the common prime factors.
4. Multiply the common factors using the smallest exponent for each.

### Example
For $m=60$ and $n=24$:
- $60 = 2^2 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 5^1$
- $24 = 2^3 \cdot 3^1$
- Common factors: $2^2$ and $3^1$
- $\text{GCD} = 2^2 \cdot 3^1 = 4 \cdot 3 = 12$
