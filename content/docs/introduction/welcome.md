# Welcome to Algobook

Algobook is an interactive platform designed to help you master algorithms through clear explanations and functional visualizations. This curriculum is designed to take you from the fundamentals of algorithm design to advanced optimization techniques.

### 🚀 Key Features
- **Interactive Visualizations**: Watch algorithms step-by-step to understand the underlying logic.
- **Exhaustive Curriculum**: Covers everything from basic searching to NP-hard combinatorial problems.
- **Premium Design**: A clean, focused interface designed for deep learning.

### 📚 Getting Started
Select a module from the **INTRODUCTION** section on the left to begin your journey. We recommend starting with **Euclid's Algorithm** to see our interactive graphs in action.
